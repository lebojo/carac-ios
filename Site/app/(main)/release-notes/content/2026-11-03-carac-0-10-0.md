---
title: "Carac 0.10.0: iCloud sync and Apple Fitness"
---

- Your trainings and sessions now sync with iCloud across your iPhone and iPad.
- Sessions can be saved as strength training workouts in Apple Health and Fitness. Turn it on in Settings.
- The last weight of each exercise shows in the session list.
- Data backup has its own menu, separate from settings.
- Weeks now start on Monday.
- Carac is fully translated into French.
- Fixes: sessions are sorted by date, and today's sessions show up on a free day.

TestFlight
November 3, 2026

Carac 0.10.0: iCloud sync and Apple Fitness

  • Your trainings and sessions now sync with iCloud across your iPhone and iPad.
  • Sessions can be saved as strength training workouts in Apple Health and Fitness. Turn it on in Settings.
  • The last weight of each exercise shows in the session list.
  • Data backup has its own menu, separate from settings.
  • Weeks now start on Monday.
  • Carac is fully translated into French.
  • Fixes: sessions are sorted by date, and today's sessions show up on a free day.
January 24, 2026

Back up your data

  • Export all your trainings and sessions to a JSON file.
  • Import a backup to restore your data on any device.
  • Clearer messages when a backup file can't be read.
January 16, 2026

Train on any day

  • Tap a day of the week on the home screen to start its training, even if it isn't planned for today.
  • Free days show up in the week, so you see your rest days at a glance.
  • The statistics calendar only highlights the days you trained.
  • Carac now asks for confirmation before you cancel a session.
December 23, 2025

Statistics for every training

  • Each training gets its own statistics: a chart of the total weight lifted per session, total weight pulled, best session, average and average trend.
  • Preview how today's session compares while you are still training.
  • Choose between light, dark or system appearance.
November 25, 2025

Your session history

  • New statistics tab with a calendar of your sessions.
  • Each exercise shows your best set from last time, and your last values are prefilled.
  • Delete a session you logged by mistake.
  • The screen stays awake during a session.
  • A short introduction welcomes you on first launch.

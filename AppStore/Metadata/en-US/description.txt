Carac is the simplest way to plan your strength training and see yourself get stronger.

Build your trainings once, give each one its days, and Carac tells you what to lift today. During the session, log every set with a tap: your last weights are already there, so you only adjust what changed.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• Create trainings from your own exercises
• Assign them to the days you train
• Today's training is waiting for you on the home screen

LOG EVERY SET IN SECONDS
• Weight and reps with quick steppers
• Last session's values prefilled, best set shown
• Reorder exercises as you go, see what's left at a glance

WATCH YOUR PROGRESS
• Chart of the total weight lifted per session
• Best session, average and trend for every training
• A calendar with every session you completed

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICES
• Syncs privately through your own iCloud
• Save sessions as strength training workouts in Apple Fitness (optional)
• Export and import a full backup at any time
• No account, no ads, no tracking

Light or dark, in English or French, on iPhone and iPad.
